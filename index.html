Privacy Policy

My Finances Planner
Last updated: 20 November 2026

Overview

My Finances Planner is a personal finance planning app. This policy explains what data is handled and how.

Data we store

Financial information you enter — income, payments, expenses, installments, savings and goals, credit card nicknames and limits (never card numbers), salary and end-of-service details, and profiles — is stored on your devices. When iCloud sync is enabled, that data is stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit so it can sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Apple Wallet pass

When you choose Add to Wallet, the app sends a summary of the current cycle to our pass server so the pass can be created and signed: your remaining balance, savings total, number of unpaid payments, billing cycle, profile name, and each card’s nickname with its remaining and used amounts. The server uses this only to build the pass and sends it straight back to your device. It does not store this information. Nothing is sent unless you use Add to Wallet.

Exchange rates

When you refresh exchange rates, the app downloads current rates from open.er-api.com. No personal or financial information is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising.
  • We do not keep your financial data on our own servers. Your records live on your devices and in your iCloud account.

Authentication

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or PIN is processed on your device. PIN storage uses the device Keychain where implemented.

Apple services

The app may use Apple services such as iCloud/CloudKit, notifications, calendar access (if you enable export), and Wallet-related features on supported platforms. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings.

Backups and export

You can export a JSON backup from Settings. It contains all your profiles and their data. Files you save, including to iCloud Drive, are under your control.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer at: myfinancesplannerapp@outlook.com

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
